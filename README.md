# Customer Support Operations Analytics

## Project Overview
An end-to-end analytics project focused on customer support performance, operational efficiency, ticket resolution and customer satisfaction. The dashboard helps identify workload patterns, service bottlenecks and opportunities to improve support operations.

## Objectives
- Monitor overall ticket volume and open tickets.
- Analyse monthly ticket trends and ticket distribution across support queues.
- Evaluate average first-response time and resolution time.
- Compare customer satisfaction across support queues.
- Monitor ticket reopening rates and ticket status.
- Compare resolution performance across queue and priority combinations.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** — interactive dashboard and data visualisation
- **SQL** — data querying and analysis
- **Python** — data analysis and preprocessing

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Customer Support Command Center
- KPI cards for total tickets, open tickets, first-response time, resolution time, CSAT and reopen rate.
- Monthly ticket-volume trend.
- Ticket workload by queue.
- Customer satisfaction by queue.
- Resolution time by priority.
- Interactive filters for channel, queue, priority and date range.

### 2. Operations Insights
- Resolution-time matrix by queue and priority.
- Ticket volume by channel.
- Ticket-status distribution.
- Key business findings and operational observations.

## Key Findings
- The Technical queue handled the highest ticket volume, with **1,282 tickets**.
- Low-priority tickets had the longest average resolution time, at **109.07 hours**.
- The Technical queue recorded the lowest average CSAT, at **3.85/5**.
- The overall ticket reopen rate was **7.13%**.

## Business Value
The analysis helps support teams understand workload distribution, investigate lengthy resolutions, monitor customer experience and identify areas for process improvement.
